The map is the page

Today the stage is one of several boxes on the page: at 1920×1080 the Map segment gives it 37.5 % of the screen, at 1280×720 22.7 %. This proposal keeps one 44 px bar and hands everything else to the map. Panels dock, fold to a rail, or float on the stage; the toolbars become one strip on the stage's edge; the legend rides on the map.

Every frame below is a static picture drawn with the demo scenario's seven fictional items. The map artwork stands in for the user's own image and is the same in both themes, as it would be in use.

Map segment · desktop 1440×900

One bar, one strip, one docked list

The two toolbars become a tool strip on the stage. The legend and In view float on the map and fold away. The offline notice is a pill in the bar, not a row. Selection is violet, so the active tab can no longer be mistaken for a green marker.

Presentation · Timeline · desktop 1440×900

The schedule, on the same ground

The ruled pins are kept as they are: 28 px lanes, the two-state line (name opens the card, chevron expands), the hatched work phase, the 2 px dashed today line, events as hairlines with chips, the used-only legend. Only the surroundings change.

Phone · 390×844

Top bar, stage, tool bar

The header drops from 101 px to 48 px by putting the segments in the bar. The two 44 px toolbar rows become one 56 px bar at the thumb. Schedule lines stay 48 px with 40 px headings; touch targets stay 44 px or more.

The two named extremes · shown in your theme

3440×1440 and 1280×720

On the ultrawide both panels dock and stay a fixed width; every extra pixel goes to the map and nothing is centred in a column. In the 1280×720 window the list folds to a 44 px rail and In view folds to its heading, and the page no longer scrolls.

Document · A4 landscape

A page that reads at arm's length

The map takes the page; the legend and the numbered list sit in one column beside it. Nothing on the page is set below 9 pt. Today's timeline figure uses 7 pt.

The system behind the frames
